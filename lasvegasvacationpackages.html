What is Included in a Las Vegas Package?

While package offerings vary depending on the platform, a typical Las Vegas vacation package bundles the fundamental elements of your trip:

  • Roundtrip Airfare: Flights into Harry Reid International Airport (LAS) integrated directly into your itinerary.
  • Hotel Accommodations: Rooms at prime Strip resorts, historic Downtown casino hotels, or off-Strip properties.
  • Optional Add-Ons: Rental cars, airport transfers, show tickets, or dining and beverage credits offered as discounted extras during checkout.

Why Bundling Saves You Money

When hotels and airlines sell components separately, they adhere to strict minimum advertised price (MAP) rules. By bundling flights and hotels together, travel providers can obscure individual prices. This lets them pass along deep wholesale savings—often reducing the total package price by hundreds compared to booking separately.

3 Essential Things to Watch Out For Before Booking

While vacation packages offer substantial value, failing to read the fine print can ruin an otherwise great deal:

1. Resort Fees Are Rarely Included in Upfront Prices

Even if a vacation package advertises a complete "Flight + Hotel" price, mandatory resort fees (typically $30–$55+ per night plus tax) are almost always collected directly by the hotel at check-in or check-out. Always account for resort fees when calculating your total vacation budget.

2. Strict Cancellation and Change Policies

Package deals combine airline ticket rules with hotel reservation terms. Basic economy airline tickets in a cheap bundle may be non-refundable and non-changeable. Always verify whether your package includes free cancellation or flexible booking options prior to checkout.

3. Blackout Dates and Event Surcharges

Targeting peak travel times—such as major conventions, holiday weekends, or marquee sporting events—can result in package prices spiking dramatically or specific properties opting out of package promotions.

⚠️ Pro Tip: Always compare package rates across multiple platforms. A package deal on one site might offer a flight discount, while another platform might include valuable resort credits or complimentary breakfast vouchers.
