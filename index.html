◆ The smart booking checklist

Keep more money for
the Vegas part.

A low nightly rate does not always mean a low final bill. These four checks can help you choose with confidence.

01

Compare the total

Resort fees, taxes, parking and cancellation terms can change the real cost of a stay.

02

Bundle, then verify

Flight-and-hotel packages may save money, but compare the package total with booking separately.

03

Match the neighborhood

The Strip is convenient for first visits; Downtown can offer value and a different Vegas atmosphere.

04

Book flexible rates

When prices are close, a refundable option gives you room to recheck rates before your trip.
